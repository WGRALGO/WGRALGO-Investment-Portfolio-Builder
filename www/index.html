About

Investment Portfolio Builder is a free investing game and planner from WGRALGO / The Wealth Gap Resolution Algorithm™ Inc. Ride 40 years of market ups and downs, then plan your own investing future with honest numbers.

How it works

  • Market Rollercoaster (the game): pick how you invest at 25, then make real decisions through crashes, hot tips, fees, and raises until 65. See how you did against Steady Sam and Diversified Dana.
  • My Investing Plan (the calculator): enter your age, contributions, employer match, and fees to see a typical, a bad, and a good outcome, in today's dollars too.

For education only, not financial advice. Results use simplified assumptions based on long-term historical averages. Past performance does not guarantee future results.
