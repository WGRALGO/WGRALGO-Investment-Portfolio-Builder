How It Works

Choose a portfolio style. Each style has an estimated annual return based on long-term educational averages. The app applies that return to your monthly contributions and any starting balance, compounded yearly until your retirement age. It then adjusts the final number for 3% annual inflation so you can see purchasing power in today's dollars.

What you'll need

  • Monthly contribution amount
  • Current age and retirement age
  • Optional starting balance
  • A portfolio style you'd like to model
