# Privacy

WGRALGO Investment Portfolio Builder does not collect personal information.

The app does not require an account, does not use analytics, does not contain ads, does not include trackers, does not upload your inputs, and does not use a backend server.

## What stays on your device

- No account required
- No ads
- No analytics
- No trackers
- No data selling
- No cloud upload
- No personal financial data is sent to WGRALGO
- Any numbers you enter (age, retirement age, monthly contribution, starting balance, portfolio selection) are used only for on-device educational simulation and remain on your device unless you manually share them via your own share/copy action.

## Network access

The APK does not require Android `INTERNET` permission. The app does not include or call third-party servers, advertising SDKs, or analytics SDKs.

## External links

If any external links remain anywhere in documentation, those external websites are separate from the APK and may have their own privacy policies. The APK interface itself contains no external links, social media links, or donation links.
